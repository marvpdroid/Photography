Calibration — once, then forget it

Meter the same grey card with both in the same light. If the 7D says 1/60 and the Sekonic says 1/45, the 7D is ⅓ stop optimistic — put 0.33 against the 7D.

On the beach, in order
  1. Meter unfiltered. Neither the 7D nor the Sekonic can read through a 10-stop — it is below both their ranges.
  2. Focus and compose before the ND goes on. A 10-stop is black through the finder.
  3. Mirror up, locking cable release, shutter ring to B. The leaf shutter only times to 1 s.
  4. Cover the eyepiece and seat the dark slide. On a six-minute frame the finder will fog the top of the neg.
  5. Note the frame — twelve on a roll, and you will not remember which was which at the scan.
Reciprocity: Ilford's published T1.31 for HP5+/FP4+; Kodak Gold on the consumer-colour table (+1 stop at 1 s, +2 at 10 s, +3 at 100 s) — bracket a stop over. Sun times computed on the device, no signal needed. Tide from Open-Meteo's model, cached for offline — heights are relative to mean sea level, not chart datum, so use it for timing, not for depth.
